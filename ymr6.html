Who we are

YMR Marine Solutions LLP is an independent marine survey, inspection and maritime consultancy company, headquartered in Mumbai, India.

We provide professional survey and inspection services to shipowners, charterers, vessel operators, cargo interests and other stakeholders across the maritime industry.

Through technical expertise, operational experience and impartial reporting, we help our clients protect their interests and make informed decisions.

Our approach is built on accuracy, integrity, transparency and a commitment to professional excellence.

How We Work

From enquiry to delivery.
Professional at every step.

A structured approach to marine surveys and inspections, with clear communication, operational coordination and professional accountability throughout every engagement.

01

Survey Request

Share your survey requirements, vessel details, port of attendance and expected operational schedule with our team.

02

Scope & Coordination

Our operations team reviews the requirements, confirms the scope of work and coordinates surveyor attendance.

03

Survey Execution

The appointed surveyor carries out the inspection, records observations and collects relevant measurements and supporting documentation.

04

Quality Review

Survey findings, calculations and documentation are reviewed for accuracy, consistency and completeness.

05

Report Delivery
